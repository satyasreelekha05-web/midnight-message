Dear Nanna,

Two days left until your birthday. Today, I want to tell you the things I feel about you but don't say often enough — the quiet reasons I admire you.

💛
caring

Since the start of our friendship, you've taken care of me. Even when I had a small health issue, you looked after me without a second thought.

🕊️
patience

No matter how much I made you angry or how much I fussed, you never raised your voice at me. You never lost your patience with me.

🗝️
understanding

Whatever the issue is, even if it's something you don't like, you still try to understand it from my side and lower your own interest for me.

And then there was last year. I went through a really tough time, and you were right there beside me through all of it — making sure my mood was okay, making sure I still smiled. My best friend, close to my heart, thank you for standing beside me through that. I felt so much back then, I broke down more than once, but because you stayed by my side, I made it out of that situation, and I'm happy now. Thank you so much. I lo... you. ❤️

And that's exactly why I call you Nanna — because of how you care for me, how you love me, and how you take responsibility for me, exactly like a father would. That's the biggest reason.

You show love quietly — checking on me, staying patient, understanding my side even when it's hard. I notice all of it. You're not just my best friend, Nanna — you're my safe place. ❤️ — always grateful for you
