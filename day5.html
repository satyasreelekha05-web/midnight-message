Dear Nanna,

Five days left until your birthday. And before that day comes, I want to take you somewhere — all the way back to where our story actually began. Because this was never an ordinary story.

It started in pre-primary school, when we were just two kids who didn't know any better than to become friends. Three years in, life had other plans — I moved away, you changed schools, and somewhere in between, we simply lost each other.

Years passed before I saw you again. Inter 2nd year, a family function, a quiet "hi" and a small smile — nothing more than two families introducing their kids.

Then, that same year, computer class holidays. I saw you from across the room. You didn't see me at all. I never said a word.

Funny how life kept placing us in the same rooms, again and again, long before either of us realized it meant something.

College came, and so did you — same group, same section. Still, silence. Until one ordinary day in the Chemistry lab, you walked up to my team and said "hi."

That's the day our story actually began. Not pre-primary. Not the family function. That lab, that hi, that day. 🌱

two tiny hearts, holding hands before they even understood why
