One day left, Bava.

Today isn't about memories anymore — today, I want to talk about everything I hope we still get to build.

I want to travel through life with you, for as long as I live. Whatever struggles come our way, I want us to face them together, solve them together, always as one team.

I want us to first settle down in life, and then get married — happily, without rushing. Right now we just talk about our future, our dreams. But once we're married, I want us to actually live it, day by day. Whether it's love or fights, I know it'll always be you. I want to be with you. I am your happiness, and in every issue, I'll stand beside you as your support — no matter how big that issue is.

I want us to visit temples together, go to tourist places together, and see the world side by side. And someday, I want us to have two kids — because being with you is the only place I've ever felt truly happy. I don't feel that happy with anyone else.
